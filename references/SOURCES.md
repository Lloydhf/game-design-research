# Sources and claim boundaries

Selected on 20 September 2026. This is a focused reading list, not an exhaustive or systematic review. The source papers are linked, not redistributed.

| Source | Use in this project | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Hunicke, LeBlanc & Zubek, 2004: MDA](https://aaai.org/papers/ws04-04-001-mda-a-formal-approach-to-game-design-and-game-research/) | Vocabulary connecting mechanics, behavior and experience | A causal effect or recommended tutorial timing |
| [Andersen et al., 2012: The Impact of Tutorials on Games of Varying Complexity](https://grail.cs.washington.edu/projects/game-abtesting/chi2012/chi2012.pdf) | Tutorial effects varied across the tested games; motivates a local comparison | An effect size transferable to DASHER |
| [Cao & Liu, 2022: Learning to play](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.heliyon.2022.e11482) | Implicit guidance and prior experience deserve attention | That implicit guidance and contextual text are the same manipulation |

The proposed sample size, metric choices, parkour variants and practical workflow are this repository's design proposal. They are not recommendations attributed to these papers.
